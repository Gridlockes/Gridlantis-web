What's in it

A document, a conversation and a call — without switching apps

  • Pages, not text boxes

    A note fills the window like a sheet of paper. Underneath it is a stack of blocks — markdown prose, code, drawings — that you add and reorder as you write.

  • Code that runs

    Syntax highlighting for JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Rust, C/C++, Java, PHP, SQL, HTML, CSS, JSON, YAML and more. JavaScript blocks run in a sandboxed worker and print their output below.

  • Drawings on the same page

    A pen, a highlighter, an eraser, colours and a brush size. Drop in pictures, drag and resize them. Strokes appear for everyone while they are still being drawn.

  • Chats that float

    Groups and private chats open in windows you can move anywhere and minimise to a dock, so talking never takes you off the page you are writing.

  • Voice rooms

    Join a group's call in one click. Audio goes peer to peer — the server only introduces people to each other and never handles the sound.

  • Private by default

    Signing up joins you to nothing and announces you to nobody. New groups are invite-only, and you find a person by looking them up, never by browsing a list.

  • Works with no connection

    A note you have open keeps working when the network drops. What you write is kept on the device and merges back in when you are online again.

  • Take it with you

    Any note downloads as plain text or a formatted PDF, and any drawing as a PNG. Nothing you write is locked in here.

Where your notes live

On your device, or in the cloud — you pick, note by note

Every note lives in exactly one of two places, and col-lab says which one on the note itself. Moving a note between them is one button, either way.

On your device

Free

Free · forever

  • Everything above: prose, code, drawings, downloads
  • Kept in your browser's storage, or — in the desktop app — in a folder you choose
  • One plain file per note, yours to back up or sync however you like
  • Never sent to our servers
  • No sharing, no co-editing, no second device

Cloud storage

Subscription

Paid plan · price shown at checkout

  • Your notes on every machine you sign in on
  • Share a note into a group or a private chat
  • Several people writing in one note at once, live
  • An offline copy kept in the browser too, so notes still open with no connection
  • Billing handled by Stripe — card details never reach us

If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted. Cloud notes stay readable, downloadable and deletable — they simply stop taking edits until the plan is back, and col-lab offers to pull a copy onto your device. Chats, groups and voice calls are not storage and are not part of the plan: they work on both.

Get started

Open it
and write

No invitation, no waiting list. Make an account and the first page is already open in front of you.

Open col-lab

  1. Make an account

    An email address and a password. Signing up joins you to nothing and tells nobody you are here.

  2. Start writing

    Your notes are kept on your device and cost nothing. Add a code block, draw something, download it as a PDF.

  3. Bring people in, when you want to

    Subscribe to cloud storage and a note can be shared into a group, co-edited live, and opened on any machine you sign in on.

  4. Forgotten your password?

    Passwords are never recoverable, only replaceable — Forgot password? on the sign-in screen emails you a link that works once.
